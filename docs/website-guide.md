# VOLTRA Website Guide & Complete Interface Reference

This document provides an exhaustive, authoritative reference for the VOLTRA (Grid Risk Intelligence & Operations Advisor) web application, its page routes, interactive controls, visual components, data flows, and operator workflows.

---

## 1. Platform Identity & Architecture

- **Application Name**: VOLTRA — Grid Risk Intelligence & Operations Advisor
- **Mission**: Full-stack predictive grid intelligence platform for power transformer health monitoring, dissolved gas analysis (DGA), cascading blackout prevention, and proactive maintenance planning.
- **Frontend Stack**: React 19, TypeScript, TanStack Router (file-based routing), Tailwind CSS, Lucide icons, Vite.
- **Backend Stack**: FastAPI, Uvicorn, Python 3.11+, Scikit-learn (`RandomForestRegressor`, `RandomForestClassifier`), SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations), Qdrant Vector Database (hybrid dense retrieval + operational metadata filtering), Groq Cloud API (LLaMA 3.3 70B Versatile).
- **Zero Mock Data Policy**: All displayed telemetry, health scores, and risk rankings are calculated from real sensor and DGA datasets (Kaggle failure analysis and DGA benchmarks for 18 Anand District transformers).

## 2. Route Directory & Page Guide

The website employs TanStack file-based routing located under `src/frontend/src/routes/`. The primary routes and their functionality are:

### 2.1. Route `/` — Cinematic Landing & Overview
- **URL**: `/`
- **Component**: `CinematicLanding.tsx` / `index.tsx`
- **Purpose**: Welcome portal, executive introduction, and system health status.
- **Key Features**:
  - **Cinematic Hero**: Industrial dark-mode aesthetic with interactive grid particle canvas and live pulsing network nodes.
  - **Mission Briefing**: Highlights the core value proposition — shifting power utilities from reactive crisis response to proactive AI-guided asset preservation.
  - **Quick Launch Deck**: Direct access cards to `/dashboard`, `/grid`, `/predict`, `/technology`, `/stream`, and `/blackout`.
  - **Live Pulse Telemetry**: Real-time status badge showing overall fleet operational status, active transformer count (18 assets in Anand Corridor), and vector copilot readiness.

### 2.2. Route `/dashboard` — Executive Operations Dashboard
- **URL**: `/dashboard`
- **Component**: `dashboard.tsx`
- **Purpose**: High-level executive grid monitoring, risk distribution, KPI tracking, and maintenance dispatching.
- **Key Sections & Features**:
  - **KPI Telemetry Cards**:
    1. *Fleet Health Index*: Average fleet health score (0–100 scale, where <50 is normal, 50–70 is severe, and 70+ indicates critical failure).
    2. *Critical Risk Count*: Number of transformers currently operating in High or Critical risk tiers.
    3. *Average Remaining Useful Life (RUL)*: Average days remaining before required major overhaul or failure.
    4. *Fleet Grid Load (MW)*: Aggregate active transmission and distribution power load.
  - **Risk Distribution Chart**: Visual breakdown of fleet assets across 5 risk tiers (Critical, High, Medium, Low, Healthy).
  - **Critical Alerts Feed**: Immediate notification stream listing high-priority transformers (e.g. TX-115 with high-energy arcing, TX-107 with thermal decomposition), displaying gas anomalies and time-criticality.
  - **7-Day Maintenance Plan**: Automated schedule generated by the heuristic maintenance planner mapping diagnosed faults to utility action codes:
    - `ELEC-INSPECT`: Low/high energy electrical discharge check.
    - `THERM-CHECK`: Infrared thermal inspection for overheated windings/core.
    - `PD-MAP`: Acoustic/electrical partial discharge mapping.
    - `OIL-FILTER`: Oil purification and moisture removal.
    - `DEGASSING`: Vacuum degassing to remove combustible gases.
    - `REPLACE`: Critical asset swap before catastrophic tank rupture.
  - **Interactive Filter Bar**: Filter fleet by risk tier, substation district, or urgent maintenance status.

### 2.3. Route `/grid` — Interactive 2D/3D GIS Grid Map & Telemetry Explorer
- **URL**: `/grid`
- **Component**: `grid.tsx`
- **Purpose**: Geospatial visualization of the Anand District power transmission network, substation topology, and real-time asset inspection.
- **Key Sections & Features**:
  - **Geospatial Map Canvas**: Interactive 2D/3D topological map of Anand District substations (Anand Main, Vidyanagar, Karamsad, Mogar, Borsad, Umreth, Petlad, Khambhat, Tarapur).
  - **Transformer Pin Markers**: 18 transformers mapped with real-time risk color-coding:
    - *Red*: Critical Risk (HI ≥ 70, RUL ≤ 8 days, active arcing or thermal runaway).
    - *Orange*: High Risk (HI 50–70, severe fault developing).
    - *Yellow*: Medium Risk (Cautionary gas rise).
    - *Green*: Healthy / Normal operation.
  - **Feeder Lines**: Dynamic line overlays connecting substations, indicating transmission voltages (220 kV, 132 kV, 66 kV, 11 kV) and power flow direction.
  - **Slide-out Asset Inspector Drawer**:
    - Clicking any transformer pin or list item opens a comprehensive inspection drawer.
    - Displays 14 DGA sensor readings: Hydrogen (H₂), Methane (CH₄), Acetylene (C₂H₂), Ethylene (C₂H₄), Ethane (C₂H₆), Carbon Monoxide (CO), Carbon Dioxide (CO₂), Oxygen (O₂), Nitrogen (N₂), Water/Moisture content (ppm), Dielectric Rigidity (kV), Interfacial Tension, Power Factor, and Oil Temperature (°C).
    - Displays ML Model 1 Health Index, calibrated RUL days, and Model 2 Duval fault classification.
    - **"Ask Grid Advisor" Button**: One-click prompt that automatically opens the `GridAdvisorChat` copilot pre-focused on the inspected asset.
  - **Custom CSV Telemetry Upload & Synchronizer**:
    - Drag-and-drop or file selector allowing operators to upload fresh operational CSV telemetry files directly in the browser.
    - Automatically parses columns, registers the dataset, indexes records into Qdrant vector storage, and synchronizes the Grid Advisor chatbot with the uploaded data.

### 2.4. Route `/predict` — Simulation Studio & What-If Sandbox
- **URL**: `/predict`
- **Component**: `predict.tsx`
- **Purpose**: Real-time asset stress testing, scenario modeling, and predictive degradation analysis.
- **Key Sections & Features**:
  - **Interactive Gas & Sensor Sliders**:
    - Adjust key combustible gas concentrations: H₂ (0–1000 ppm), CH₄ (0–1000 ppm), C₂H₂ (0–500 ppm), C₂H₄ (0–1000 ppm), C₂H₆ (0–500 ppm), CO (0–2000 ppm), CO₂ (0–10000 ppm).
    - Adjust physical metrics: Water content (ppm), Dielectric breakdown voltage (kV), Oil temperature (°C).
  - **Instant ML Inference Engine**:
    - Real-time recalculation of Model 1 Health Index score (0–100) and RUL (days).
    - Real-time classification of Model 2 IEC 60599 Fault Type (NF, PD, D1, D2, T1, T2, T3).
  - **Interactive Duval Triangle**: Visualizes the relative percentages of %CH₄, %C₂H₄, and %C₂H₂ dynamically moving across fault zones (PD, D1, D2, T1, T2, T3).
  - **Environmental & Grid Stress Modifiers**:
    - *Load Surge Multiplier*: Simulates 1.0x to 2.5x load surges (e.g. extreme heatwaves, industrial spikes) to see accelerated insulation aging.
    - *Ambient Heatwave Slider*: Simulates thermal stress on transformer cooling systems.

### 2.5. Route `/stream` — Real-Time Telemetry Stream Monitor
- **URL**: `/stream`
- **Component**: `stream.tsx`
- **Purpose**: Live SCADA/IoT high-frequency packet telemetry monitoring and streaming health checks.
- **Key Sections & Features**:
  - **Live Stream Canvas**: Visualizes streaming packets arriving from substation RTUs (Remote Terminal Units).
  - **Time-Series Telemetry Charts**: Real-time plotting of grid load (MW), phase frequency (Hz, nominal 50.0 Hz), voltage fluctuations, and hot-spot winding temperatures.
  - **Packet Inspection Terminal**: Displays packet sequence, arrival timestamp, latency (ms), CRC integrity, and automatic anomaly flags.
  - **Stream Controls**: Pause/Resume streaming, adjust ingestion frequency (100ms to 2000ms), and simulate drop-out events.

### 2.6. Route `/blackout` — Cascading Outage & Contingency Analysis
- **URL**: `/blackout`
- **Component**: `blackout.tsx`
- **Purpose**: Grid resilience analysis, N-1 contingency simulations, and emergency mitigation response.
- **Key Sections & Features**:
  - **N-1 Contingency Simulator**: Models the catastrophic failure and emergency trip of any selected transformer (e.g. TX-115).
  - **Cascading Failure Engine**: Calculates dynamic load redistribution to neighboring transformers and feeders, highlighting secondary overload points.
  - **Socio-Economic Impact Assessment**:
    - Estimated population without power.
    - Critical infrastructure at risk (hospitals, water treatment plants, railway traction).
    - Estimated economic loss per hour of unserved energy (MWh).
  - **Mitigation Dispatch Deck**:
    - Emergency load shedding recommendations.
    - Power re-routing via parallel 132kV/66kV circuits.
    - Mobile substation deployment protocols.

### 2.7. Route `/technology` — AI Architecture & Engineering Deep-Dive
- **URL**: `/technology`
- **Component**: `technology.tsx`
- **Purpose**: Technical documentation of the underlying machine learning models, training data, validation metrics, and international engineering standards.
- **Key Sections & Features**:
  - **Model 1 — Health Index Regressor**:
    - Algorithm: `RandomForestRegressor` (100 estimators, max depth 12).
    - Training Data: Kaggle transformer failure dataset (470 real power transformer records).
    - Validation Metrics: Test R² = 0.72, MAE = 5.88.
    - 14 input features: H₂, O₂, N₂, CH₄, CO, CO₂, C₂H₄, C₂H₆, C₂H₂, DBDS, Power factor, Interfacial voltage, Dielectric rigidity, Water content.
  - **Model 2 — DGA Fault Classifier**:
    - Algorithm: `RandomForestClassifier` (100 estimators, max depth 10).
    - Training Data: Kaggle DGA dataset (4,150 real DGA records).
    - Validation Metrics: Accuracy = 90.8%, Macro F1 = 0.896.
    - 7 IEC 60599 Fault Classes:
      1. `NF` — No Fault (Baseline gas levels)
      2. `PD` — Partial Discharge (High H₂, CH₄)
      3. `D1` — Low-Energy Electrical Discharge (Sparking, C₂H₂, H₂)
      4. `D2` — High-Energy Electrical Discharge (Arcing, C₂H₂, C₂H₄)
      5. `T1` — Thermal Fault < 300°C (CH₄, CO)
      6. `T2` — Thermal Fault 300–700°C (C₂H₄, CH₄)
      7. `T3` — Thermal Fault > 700°C (C₂H₄, C₂H₆)
  - **Duval Triangle Feature Engineering**: Ratios calculated as %CH₄ = CH₄/(CH₄ + C₂H₄ + C₂H₂), %C₂H₄ = C₂H₄/(CH₄ + C₂H₄ + C₂H₂), %C₂H₂ = C₂H₂/(CH₄ + C₂H₄ + C₂H₂).
  - **IEEE C57.104 dissolved gas threshold tables**: Outlines Condition 1 (Normal), Condition 2 (Caution), Condition 3 (High Risk), and Condition 4 (Extreme Danger).
  - **SHAP Attribution Engine**: Explains individual model predictions using TreeSHAP to reveal which dissolved gases contributed most to the risk score.

### 2.8. Route `/login` — Role-Based Access Control & Authentication
- **URL**: `/login`
- **Component**: `login.tsx`
- **Purpose**: Secure operator authentication and role assignment.
- **Supported Roles**:
  1. `Admin`: Full system control, dataset indexing, model retraining triggers, and user management.
  2. `Grid Operator`: Real-time monitoring, maintenance scheduling, incident logging, and emergency dispatch.
  3. `Risk Analyst`: Telemetry analytics, what-if simulations, and data export.
- **Session Persistence**: Stores authenticated session in browser `localStorage` (`voltra_auth_user`) for seamless navigation across routes.

## 3. Global Components & Interactive Widgets

### 3.1. `GridAdvisorChat` — Standalone RAG Copilot Drawer
- Accessible from every page via the bottom-right floating badge or top navigation bar.
- Direct dual-domain RAG integration: Answers queries grounded strictly in active operational telemetry and VOLTRA project/website documentation.
- Suggested prompt pills for quick navigation and fleet inquiry.
- Automatic context synchronization: When inspecting an asset in `/grid`, the chat automatically synchronizes with that asset ID.
- Source attribution cards: Cites exact document names, sections, asset IDs, and telemetry readings.

### 3.2. `SiteNav` — Navigation Header
- Persistent top navigation bar.
- Route links: Dashboard, Grid, Predict, Stream, Blackout, Technology.
- Real-time connection beacon (indicates live backend and RAG API connectivity).
- Active dataset badge: Displays the current active telemetry dataset (e.g. `Anand Corridor (Sample)` or custom uploaded CSV).
- User profile badge with quick logout and role indicator.

### 3.3. `TX115InterventionBanner` — Emergency Asset Alert Banner
- High-visibility amber/red banner triggered when critical assets (such as TX-115) exceed safe DGA operating thresholds.
- Provides one-click access to the emergency mitigation workflow and immediate operator dispatch.

### 3.4. `IncidentReportModal` — Community & Field Incident Filing
- Modal dialog allowing field lineworkers and local observers to report physical anomalies.
- Form fields: Substation/Location, Asset ID, Incident Type (Audible Buzzing/Hum, Oil Leakage, Smoke/Fire, Sparking, Physical Damage), Severity, Description, and photo attachment.
- Submissions are logged to the backend incident register for automated dispatch review.

### 3.5. `DataSourceBadge` & `EmptyWorkspaceChoice`
- Displays the active operational data source and allows seamless switching between the default Anand Corridor 18-transformer baseline and operator-uploaded CSV telemetry files.
- Provides visual confirmation of data freshness and record count.

## 4. UI Design Standards & Color Palette

- **Theme**: Cybernetic Industrial Dark Mode.
- **Background**: Deep obsidian/charcoal (`#080C14`, `#0B1120`, `#111827`).
- **Surface & Cards**: Glassmorphism with subtle border glow (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
- **Accents**:
  - *Neon Cyan / Blue* (`#06B6D4`, `#3B82F6`): Systems nominal, data streaming, navigation.
  - *Emerald Green* (`#10B981`): Healthy status (HI < 50), verified integrity.
  - *Amber / Yellow* (`#F59E0B`): Cautionary condition, Medium risk (HI 50–70).
  - *Electric Red / Crimson* (`#EF4444`): Critical alarm, arcing, HI ≥ 70, emergency trip.
  - *Purple / Violet* (`#8B5CF6`): Machine learning inference, SHAP attributions, RAG copilot.
- **Typography**: Inter / Outfit modern sans-serif with monospace font for sensor telemetry, asset codes, and gas ppm values.
